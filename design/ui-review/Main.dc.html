Four defects found, and the pattern behind each
FIXED
Read did nothing, forever
Three refusal paths returned silently, and a stuck state had no timeout. One stray gesture killed the button for the session.
Pattern: a control that can refuse must say why.
FIXED
The way in fired a recall
The overlay opens on a both-encoder hold, so RECALL's timer began already held and fired 250 ms later — bus-wide.
Pattern: an entry gesture must not survive into the screen it opens.
FIXED
The scan froze the instrument
Not the scan — its logging. Serial output blocks up to 120 ms per line when the host isn't reading. 61 lines, 57 ms stalls, dead UI.
Pattern: diagnostics must not cost more than the work.
OPEN
Modules are addresses, not names
You dial hex to choose a module, and a scan is part of the everyday path rather than setup.
Pattern: the instrument should remember what's in the case.
Six artboards: two whole-instrument, four on the 200e app
01
Open on your case, not on an address. A remembered list of modules by name. Scan becomes setup you run when hardware changes.
02
Say why an action is unavailable, in place. The action row already knows; it just doesn't tell you until you press.
03
Make the write's blast radius the headline. A save rewrites all 30 presets. That fact currently shares a line with everything else.
04
Make release-first a global rule. Every screen opened by a chord can be entered with its own buttons still held. One of those already fired a bus-wide recall.
