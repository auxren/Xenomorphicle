PROPOSAL 01
Open on your case, not on an address
Scanning is setup, not a step you repeat. Remember what answered, and let the list be the way in.
Now
200e Modules
Addr 5C  ~251 A

scan: 12/61
found 2
L:scan D:probe  2 hit
You dial a hex address with the right encoder. The scan runs in the everyday path and takes up to a minute — a full second per address that doesn't answer. Nothing is remembered, so it runs again next time.
Proposed
200e Modules
>251e        5C
 259e A      28
 210         20

encR:open  L:rescan
Your three modules, by name, remembered across reboots in one 64-bit word. The address moves to the right — still visible, no longer the handle. Rescan is a deliberate act for when hardware changes.
Why it earns the rows
The list replaces the address field, the progress line and the hint line — three rows spent on a process, returned to content. Names are already known: identification on this bus is an address→model table, so the instrument can name what it found without asking anything.
The tradeoff, honestly
A remembered module that has since been unplugged still appears — you find out when a read fails. Verifying the list on every open would just be the scan again, wearing a different hat. Rescan is the cure, and it stays one press away.
A clone squats the address of whatever it emulates — the CSR answers at 0x20 as a “210”. The name is a strong hint, never a proof, so the screen should keep showing the address beside it rather than hiding it.
