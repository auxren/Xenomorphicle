PROPOSAL 03
Lead with the blast radius
A save rewrites all thirty presets, not the one you edited. That is the single most important fact on the screen, and it currently shares a line.
Now
SAVE to 251e @5C
Slot 10   1 byte

Rewrites ALL 30 slots
A:confirm  encL:back
Correct and complete. But the sentence that should stop you sits in body position, in the same weight as everything else, one line above the button that does it.
Proposed
██ REWRITES ALL 30 ██
251e @5C
1 byte differs
slot 10 - stage 4
A:confirm  encL:back
The consequence is the headline, inverted so it reads as a banner rather than a sentence. Then what changes, then how much, then where. Same rows, reordered by what could hurt.
What is already right, and should not be traded away
The changed-byte count is shown. “1 byte differs” is a real check against a mistake — a number in the thousands means the wrong bank, not the wrong stage.
Arm, then confirm. Two deliberate presses, and the guard re-runs at commit rather than trusting the verdict from when it was armed.
A nothing-to-do write is refused. Zero changed bytes says so and skips, rather than pushing 63,120 bytes for no reason.
Stale reads cannot be written. The bank must have come from this module, this session, at full length — a short read blocks the write outright.
The failure this is guarding against
One real read came back 957 bytes instead of 990 — one record short — while reporting success with no error. Harmless on a read. Behind a write, it would have shifted every subsequent record and rewritten twenty-nine presets that were never touched, silently. The guards exist because that has already happened once.
