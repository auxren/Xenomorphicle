Everything here is provisional. The Buchla 251e byte format and the Xenomorpher's SysEx framing are both still being designed/reverse-engineered by firmware in parallel -- see the header comments in sequence-codec.js and sysex-transport.js for exactly what's placeholder and why. This applet is built and tested against the Mock transport's synthetic dump; the live 200e bus isn't yet cleared as electrically safe to attach, so WebMIDI mode is untested against real hardware. Add ?transport=webmidi to the URL to preselect it.
Voltage 0–10V per step, quantized to ~78 mV (7-bit placeholder encoding). Gate column toggles whether a step fires its trigger output. Sequence length trims how many steps play before the sequence wraps.
